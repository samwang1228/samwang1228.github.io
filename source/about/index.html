Background & Vision 心路歷程與使命

From Canvas to Code 從畫筆到程式碼

Navigating life with a respirator and turning resilience into technological innovation. 帶著呼吸器勇闖人生，將不屈的生命韌性轉化為改變世界的科技力量。

My Journey 生命旅程

At age 12, an acute viral infection severely paralyzed my motor nerves, making me reliant on a medical respirator. Instead of giving in, I learned to hold a paintbrush and expressed my optimism on canvas, which was recognized with the 2016 Presidential Education Award. 12 歲那年，一場罕見病毒感染重創了我的運動神經，使我必須終身依賴呼吸器。面對命運的考驗，我未曾言棄，學會用雙手握緊畫筆，在畫布上彩繪出熱愛生命的色彩，並於 2016 年榮獲總統教育獎（藝術領域）。

Later, I channeled that same relentless determination into computer science. Delving into embedded systems, operating system kernels, and AI, I graduated top of my class at Chang Gung University and pursued my master's degree at NTUST. In 2023, I was deeply honored to receive the Presidential Education Award for the second time, this time in science and technology. 求學路上，我將這份毅力注入由 0 與 1 組成的資訊世界。深入鑽研嵌入式作業系統核心、硬體驅動與人工智慧，以系排第二的優異成績自長庚大學資工系畢業，並進入臺科大資工所深造。2023 年，我深感榮幸能第二度榮獲總統教育獎（科技領域）。

"My mission is to unite low-level firmware engineering with machine learning to build intelligent assistive devices that decode neurological and physiological signals—empowering individuals with neuromuscular conditions to regain independence and live better lives." 「我的使命是融合軟韌體底層工程與機器學習，研發能判定人體腦神經與肌肉訊號的智能輔具，讓受神經受損與肌無力所苦的病友重獲生活自主與希望。」

Storage Systems & FTL 快閃記憶體系統與 FTL 演算法

SAM: Adaptive Management for TLC NAND Flash SAM: TLC 快閃記憶體自適應管理架構 (碩士論文)

Master's Thesis (2025) 國立臺灣科技大學碩士論文 (2025)

Architecture Diagram 系統架構圖

source/about/thesis_arch.png

Characterized threshold voltage (Vth) shifts on commercial TLC/QLC NAND; designed the SAM FTL integrating 2-level LRU hot/cold data separation, sliding-window WOM-v switching, and voltage-aware page allocation. Engineered dual-threshold GC and heap-based intra/inter-area wear-leveling (0.009% metadata overhead). Validated on MSR enterprise traces, achieving 100% workload completion, 68.8% fewer erasures, and up to 36.6% RBER reduction (0–3,000 P/E). 針對商業 TLC/QLC NAND 進行臨界電壓（Vth）位移特徵分析，設計整合雙層 LRU 冷熱分離、滑動窗口 WOM-v 切換與電壓感知分頁分配的 SAM 快閃記憶體轉換層（FTL）。開發雙門檻垃圾回收（Dual-Threshold GC）與基於堆積（Heap）的區域磨損平衡演算法（僅 0.009% 額外元數據開銷）；經由 MSR 企業級負載驗證，達成 100% 工作負載完成率、抹除次數減少 68.8% 及高達 36.6% 的原始誤碼率（RBER）降低。

Flash Memory (FTL) NAND Reliability Wear-Leveling Dual-Threshold GC MSR Enterprise Traces C / C++
